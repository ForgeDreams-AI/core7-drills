Captain Side Plug

Taking the hydrant from the captain's side of the engine. Walk the eight steps in order, the way the academy teaches it. No grading — just the steps, in order, until they stick.

The standard: water must reach the clamp within 1 minute 15 seconds, and all tasks within 3 minutes 40 seconds. Learn the steps first, then run it against the clock.

The eight steps

  1. Dismount — complete stop, look both ways, step off
  2. Grab your gear — wrench in right hand, pack strap + Humat belt with left
  3. Move away — pivot right, hose over left shoulder, never backward, 7 strides
  4. The five strides — pivot toward the plug, 5 strides past it, squat + signal
  5. To the hydrant — flip the hose over the hydrant, Humat out of the work area
  6. Hook up the Humat — break the cap, wrench on the stem, Humat on the threads, CLOSED
  7. Charge it — stand behind, check, open completely, water in 1:15, clamp
  8. Finish up — wrench back, SCBA, tool, fire — inside 3:40
