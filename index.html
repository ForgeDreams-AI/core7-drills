SCBA

Donning the self-contained breathing apparatus. Walk the seven steps in order, the way the academy teaches it. No grading — just the steps, in order, until they stick.

The standard: donning the SCBA must be completed in 35 seconds. Learn the steps first, then run it against the clock.

The seven steps

  1. Valve on — flip the pack upside down, cylinder valve fully on, listen for Vibralert + PASS
  2. Over the head — lift the pack overhead, lower it onto your back
  3. Straps — shoulder straps, buckle + tighten the waist belt
  4. Facepiece — pull it on from the bag at your left hip, secure the harness
  5. Seal check — press the mask to your face, check the seal
  6. Hood + helmet — flash hood up, helmet on, all skin covered
  7. Regulator — connect it, start the air. Air comes last.
